Ingredient audit · 4 servings, ~2 lb thighs

Parallel cook plan

  1. Night before, or morning: yogurt-spice marinade on thighs. Freeze flat if meal-prepping.
  2. While chicken rests out of the fridge: bloom whole spices in oil or ghee, sweat onion, garlic, ginger.
  3. Sear chicken in a second pan while the masala simmers with tomato and paste.
  4. Start basmati (rinse, soak 15 min, then simmer) during the sauce simmer.
  5. Finish sauce with butter or yogurt and a splash of acid. Naan in a dry skillet last 4 minutes.

Active time about 35 minutes if sear and rice overlap. Pressure-cooker path: 12 minutes high pressure after the sear, natural release 8.
